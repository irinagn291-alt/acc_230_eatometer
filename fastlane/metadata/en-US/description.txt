Eatometer is a personal food log for tracking daily energy and macros. It is not medical advice. Nutrition figures come from Open Food Facts.

• Onboarding introduces the app and lets you set daily dials for Energy kcal, Protein g, Carbs g and Fat g, or skip with factory dials
• Reading drum shows today’s energy remaining against your target, macro totals, and four slots: Reading I, Reading II, Reading III and Spot Check
• Catalog search finds specimens by name with debounced lookup against Open Food Facts, falling back to a bundled local shelf
• Optical pickup scans package barcodes with VisionKit, or accepts a typed code and local sample specimens when no camera is available
• Take measurement shows per-100 g macros, a Grams field, live stroke totals, slot selection, Eaten today or a Horizon date up to 14 days ahead, watchlist marking, and Commit measurement
• Watchlist stores barcode-unique specimens for quick measurement later
• Log lists recorded strokes by day with Prev and Next navigation, deletion, Mark eaten today for planned strokes, and a 14-day horizon view
• Analytics drum shows 7, 30 and 90-day energy trends, weekday averages, a macro split donut, adherence to the energy dial, and best and worst days
• Targets lets you write dials, re-run onboarding, reset all data, and contact eatometer.pro

All readings, targets and watchlist marks stay on your device in local storage.
